Ten ways to build the Qoogies home page

Each template is a full, clickable home page built from Brand Guide v2.0: the same logo, colors, fonts, characters and taglines, told ten different ways. Every template has a Worlds section: tap any place, ship or character to open its file.

Bubble Sphere

Template 1

Deep underwater glass panels, straight from the guide's website chapter. Bubbles rise the whole way down and pop when you tap them. The page turns dusty lime when Doc Neon shows up.

Best for: the main fan site and watching the pilot.

What they share

Mix and match freely

  • Same brand tokens. Lilita One, Exo 2 and Nunito; Deep Space, Aqua Glow and one lime button per screen.
  • Same story. Team Aero makes rain, Doc Neon wants deserts, and "Waves Connect Worlds." signs off every page.
  • Two pop-ups, ten looks. Every character opens the same profile, and every place, ship and pilot moment opens the same Worlds file (13 of them, with pilot timecodes and colors), each styled to match its template.
  • Sections swap between templates. The character shelf from 4, the planet map from 5, the ship cutaway from 9 or the search from 8 can drop into any other design.
  • Nothing for sale. No shop, prices or bag anywhere. Episodes 4 to 14, five of the six target planets, the bubble sphere and the Queen are not designed yet. The planet readings in 3 and 5 are placeholders, and pilot frames still carry the XBX bug.
