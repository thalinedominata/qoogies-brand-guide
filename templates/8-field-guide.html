Places & ships

Two sides of the same galaxy. On the left, everything made of water and light. On the right, everything made of metal and smoke.

Our side (wet)

Round, soft, glowing from inside. No rivets, no weapons.

Their side (dry)

Pointed, hard, glowing only from exhaust. Lime is a spice.

The target list

Size chart

How tall is a Qoogie?

Measured in heads. A Qoogie is about 2.5 heads tall, so one would reach about up to your hip. Tap anyone to meet them.

2.5 headsOne head for the bubble, one and a half for body and legs. Never count hair or hats.

Doc Neon: 2.7A little taller than the Qoogies, plus antennae, so he can look down his nose at them.

You: about 7.5An average grown-up human is about seven and a half heads tall.
