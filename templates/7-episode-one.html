Previously, in the galaxy

Some worlds need rain. Someone doesn’t want them to have it.

The heroes

The Qoogies

Nomadic hydro-formers from the great bubble sphere. Wisp and Team Aero travel the galaxy making rain for arid worlds.

The villain

Doc Neon

A brilliant, irritable scientist of the same bubble species who thinks it’s his duty to keep every world dry. He sends his Neonite clones to stop the Qoogies.

The fight

Water versus dry, told with bubbles.

Cool, glowing water against hot, acidic neon. In Episode 1, it all starts on a dusty orange planet called Arridia.
