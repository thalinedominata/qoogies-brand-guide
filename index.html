ブランドChapter 1 · The brand

Water versus dry, told with bubbles

The Qoogies, a race of hydro-formers, and Doc Neon, a creator of arid worlds, vie for galactic dominance as each tries to transform worlds to their liking.

The Qoogies are nomadic hydro-formers from the great bubble sphere. They have made rain for arid worlds for as long as anyone can remember, and in doing so have saved many civilizations without meaning to.

Doc Neon, a brilliant and irritable scientist of the same bubble species, believes it is his duty to keep those worlds dry. He sends clones of himself and his Neonites across the galaxy to stop the Qoogies.

Everything in the brand comes from that tension: cool, glowing water against hot, acidic neon. Heroes glow aqua and iridescent. The villain glows lime. The two never share a color role.

Brand promise

Waves Connect Worlds.

The sign-off already printed on the Wisp, Hydro and Buoy sheets. Use it as the primary tagline. "Brighter Worlds. Together." is the secondary line.

Pillar

Teamwork makes the weather

No Qoogie solves an episode alone. Every role (leader, tension, hyper wave, mixology, interface) is one part of making rain.

Pillar

Curious science

Waves, mixtures, tension and signals are real ideas the show can play with. Science is fun and a little bit messy.

Pillar

Optimism with a splash

Every character line points to a brighter tomorrow. Even a retreat ends with "we are leaving in peace."

Pillar

A villain you like to watch

Doc Neon is grumpy, vain and often in disguise. He loses with style and sometimes has the time of his life.

タイトルロゴChapter 2 · Logo

One logo going forward

There are two logos in the files. The 2026 character sheets use a lime bubble logo. The 2022 pitch guide uses a pink and orange one. Lead with the lime logo everywhere new.

Primary  2026 bubble logo. Lime gel letters, yellow highlight, deep blue outline, floating bubbles. It matches the 3D bubble-form look and the hero palette.

Legacy  2022 pitch logo. Keep for archive and investor history only. Its pink and orange do not appear in the new character art, so mixing the two logos will split the brand.

Clear space

Keep a margin equal to the height of the "Q" bowl on every side. Nothing else goes inside it: no text, no characters, no UI.

Size and placement

  • Minimum width 140 px on screen, 1.25 in (32 mm) in print.
  • Website header: 160–200 px wide. Hero: up to 560 px.
  • Best grounds: Deep Space, Abyss, or an underwater blue gradient.
  • On light grounds, keep the logo on its blue bubble plate.
  • Lock up with the XBX Entertainment mark below or to the right, at no more than 40% of the logo's height.

Files we still need

All logo copies here are raster crops from sheets. Before the website launches, get a master vector (SVG/AI) plus transparent PNGs at 512, 1024 and 2048 px, and a one-color white version for video bugs and merch.

美術・スタイルChapter 4 · Style

3D bubble form leads, 2D supports

The 2026 sheets define the show's look: glossy, translucent toy-like characters lit from inside. The 2022 pitch art and storyboards show a 2D cartoon line that is still useful for boards, merch and activities.

Bubble-form rules

  1. Translucent gel skin. Light passes through the head and hands. You can see small bubbles suspended inside.
  2. Iridescent core. A rainbow sheen (cyan, lime, yellow, pink, violet) blooms across cheeks and chest. Strongest under the chin and in the hands.
  3. Wet specular highlights. One big soft highlight and a few tiny sharp ones on every rounded form.
  4. Chibi proportions. Head is about 40–45% of total height. Characters are smaller than an average human and look like collectible figures.
  5. Solid, matte clothing. Jackets, hats and shoes read as vinyl, leather and canvas. They are opaque so the gel skin stands out.
  6. Cool rim light. Aqua rim light on heroes. Lime glow on Doc Neon. Red point lights on Neonites.

Environments

Labs and control rooms are dark navy with cyan holograms and warm amber accents (see Project Aquafy). Space is deep blue with a nebula glow. Arid worlds like Arridia should go warm and dusty so the Qoogies' blue reads as water arriving.

Qoogies are 2.5 heads tall. One head for the bubble, one and a half for body and legs. Heelo's spikes add a tenth on top; never count hair or hats.

Doc Neon is a little taller at 2.7 heads, plus antennae. He should always look down at the Qoogies.

An average human is about 7.5 heads. A Qoogie stands roughly at a human's hip, matching the size comparisons on the sheets.

Bubble physics · squash and stretch

Movement rules

  • Everything Qoogie squashes. Landings flatten to about 125 × 75%, take-offs stretch to 85 × 118%. Volume stays constant.
  • Ease in, ease out. Spacing bunches at the top of a hop (the ticks on the left), like a bubble slowing in water.
  • Secondary jiggle. Hair, hats and antennae lag one or two frames behind the head.
  • Doc Neon is stiffer. Less squash, sharper stops. When he gets wet, he loses all structure and melts.

パイロットChapter 5 · From the pilot

What Episode 1 adds

Checked against "Qoogies Pilot Episode 1" (14 min 28 s, 1080p) and the pilot demo script by Joe Caruso. The pilot matches the 3D bubble-form style and hero palette in this guide. It also adds a few things the guide now covers.

Dry-world and alert colors (new)

New brand marks seen on screen

  • Q monogram. A rounded "Q:" mark on the ship's floors and screens. Make it the favicon, app icon and social avatar.
  • Neonite crest. A circle split by a spear, on the banners "Clones for a dry galaxy". Use it for villain pages only.
  • Doc Neon name plate. "Doc Neon · Chief Scientist" with an angry antenna face. Good merch detail.
  • "We come in peace" cap. Wisp's cap from the script. A ready-made merch item.

Story and world details

  • Target planet list on the ship: Veldra, Terralis, Solen, Arridia, Kaelith, Nova-9.
  • Doc Neon's base is an underground cavern. His "Neometer" screen and "halo pad" are his tools.
  • Doc Neon can melt into lime slime. Water-against-dry is literal: keep slime on the villain side.
  • New supporting creatures: brown, wide-eyed caterpillar servers that carry food trays. They need names and a sheet.

Using pilot footage

  • The XBX bug is burned into the bottom-right of every frame. Export clean frames from the project files for the website and key art.
  • The XBX opener (logo over a planet) is a good 3-second sting for trailers.
  • Website hero loop: 8–12 seconds from the launch bay or orbit shots, muted, with captions on the full player.

What it tells us

The show lives in blue. About 80% of the pilot reads blue. That's the brand's home color, and the site should feel the same.

Warm means danger. Orange only shows in the cold open and builds from 10:00 as the rockets launch and the alarms go off. Keep warm light for conflict.

Lime is a spice. Doc Neon's green barely registers frame-wide, so it pops whenever he's on screen. Don't flood whole scenes or web pages with it.

メカ設定Chapter 8 · Ships

Two fleets you can tell apart in a second

Qoogie ships are made of water and light. Doc Neon's rockets are made of metal and smoke. Keep that contrast in every shot, toy and web graphic.

Team Aero

The Qoogie ship

Hydro-forming cruiser

Silhouette key. A long, rounded glass hull with three bubble-pod engines at the back and a dome on top. You can see through it: crew pods, labs and water tanks glow inside.

Inside. Command Center (Wisp's chair), Launch Bay 01 with the ground crew, Hatch A, and Dr. Nonni Nunu's lab. Signage uses big single letters and numbers.

Material rules. Same as the characters: translucent, iridescent, wet highlights, aqua engine glow. No hard edges, no rivets, no weapons.

Villains

Doc Neon's rockets

Neonite fleet

Silhouette key. Retro 1950s rockets: pointed nose, tail fins, a red band and Doc Neon's gold crest. They launch straight up out of a silo in his underground cavern.

Variants. Standard rocket (single body) and the heavy launcher with four lime-glowing boosters.

Material rules. Opaque painted metal, rivets, smoke and dust. Lime glow only from engines and tanks. Never glass, never iridescent.

Hero vs villain shapes

  • Qoogie: round, horizontal, soft, glowing from inside.
  • Neon: pointed, vertical, hard, glowing only from exhaust.
  • In the same frame, keep the Qoogie ship cooler and brighter.

On the website

  • A "World" page section for each fleet, using the cutaway as an interactive tour.
  • The Qoogie ship flying past is a good loading animation and 404 page.
  • Use clean renders without the XBX bug.

Still to define

  • Ship names. The pilot doesn't name either vessel.
  • A turnaround sheet for each ship, like the character sheets.
  • Heelo's spacesuit, which first appears in the pilot.

声とトーンChapter 9 · Voice

How the Qoogies sound

Bright, curious and kind, with a wink. Short lines that a kid can repeat on the playground.

Taglines

  • Waves Connect Worlds. Primary. End cards, site footer, trailers.
  • Brighter Worlds. Together. Secondary. Team moments, campaigns.
  • Arid Worlds. Eternal Conflict. Villain-side promos.
  • Different Worlds. A Drier Tomorrow. Doc Neon parody line.

Hero voice

  • Upbeat and plain-spoken. Short sentences.
  • Talks about "we" more than "I".
  • Water words used lightly: wave, splash, flow, brew.
  • The "Qoog-" prefix is the show's naming game (Qoogsploration, Qoogie Board). Use it for episodes and features, not every sentence.

Villain voice

  • Dry, grumpy, deadpan. Thinks water is rude.
  • Never scary for real. The joke is that he cares too much about dust.
  • Neonites speak in short creed lines: "No more water."

Say

"Meet Heelo. He keeps the team together when things get wobbly."

"New episode: Doc Neon throws a dance party. It does not go to plan."

"Watch the pilot. Then tell us your favorite Qoogie."

Avoid

"Experience an immersive hydro-forming adventure franchise."

"Doc Neon will destroy everything you love."

"Qoogtastic Qoogie Qoogventures await!"

未決事項Chapter 11 · Open items

Things to settle before launch

  • Team Aero (settled). Aero is the team's name, not a character. Fix the synopses for "Qoogsploration" and "Qoogie And Sons": "Aero and his team" becomes "Wisp and Team Aero".
  • Logo. Confirm the lime logo replaces the 2022 one, and get vector files.
  • Arridia or Aniridia? The pilot and script say "Arridia" (and "Arridia Prime"). The 2022 pitch says "Aniridia". This guide uses Arridia.
  • On-screen typos. The alarm screen reads "UNKNOWN VESSL DETECTED". The script also has small typos ("disaproving", "technicaly") worth fixing before it goes to talent.
  • Wisp's species line (settled for now). Wisp is both a vaporous humanoid and a bubble form. Describe him as "a vaporous humanoid in bubble form" until this changes.
  • Copy fixes in the pitch. "dessert world" should be "desert world". "the Qoogie's water park" should be "the Qoogies' water park".
  • Target age. The guide assumes ages 6–11 plus parents. Confirm so the voice and site rules fit.
  • The Queen. The Queen of the Qoogies appears in two episodes but has no design yet.
